⚠️ Alert Prioritization

RakshaNet ranks a region by combining live weather signals, hazard focus and emergency context. It is an early-warning aid, not a replacement for official IMD warnings.

🔴 P1 CriticalExtreme or rapidly worsening conditions
🟠 P2 HighSerious conditions requiring preparedness
🟡 P3 WatchMonitor trends and local reports
🟢 P4 NormalNo significant live signal detected
Official-source escalation should always follow the latest IMD warning bulletin for the affected district/state.
